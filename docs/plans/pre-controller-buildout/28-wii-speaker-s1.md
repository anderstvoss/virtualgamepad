## Goal and decided context

Provide bounded, family-native speaker-data reception and decoding, with typed loss-aware host-output delivery to the application through ADR-0024. This is a source-compatible in-band subsystem, not an audio device or a guaranteed playback service. Keep the existing Wii family and `create_wii_remote(CreationOptions, WiiRemoteOptions)` at curated and ordinary-root layers (C7). Caller-specified identity at creation with per-instance defaults (#156, #162) stays unchanged; do not conflate revisions or alter identity just to admit speaker commands. This brief assumes VG-1b d/e have landed: `WiiRemoteInputs`, `inputs()`, `set_inputs`, per-field setters and `edit_inputs` over one transactional core; battery, identity, clocks and topology remain separate typed views. Speaker is host output, not caller input, not EEPROM and not another attached controller. Preserve commit/service and neutralize semantics; no compatibility aliases.

- Greatest support possible, with explicit playable versus faithful distinctions. Retain the playable reduced uinput surface and its existing rumble. Uinput has no native HID report/register channel: refuse speaker opt-in before opening any provider and document/test the deviation, without widening its native output type. No fallback to UHID.
- On `linux.uhid.bluetooth` (bus metadata only), opt in at creation to the controller-owned speaker subsystem under ADR-0023. Default off; preserve all currently accepted control and rumble behavior even when off. The opt-in is a family toggle on the existing realization, not shared UAC audio, a new generic target, or an implicit sidecar. Any consumer-only shim must have its own explicit creation-time toggle, default off.
- No fake UAC/ALSA card, implicit PipeWire sink, provider decoder, application routing engine, USB-bus UHID, USB/IP, dummy_hcd, radio/pairing/BlueZ or new IPC. Real Bluetooth remains #164. Were a distinct USB-level feature ever justified, brief 17's USB/IP production / dummy_hcd experimental-secondary precedent and separate host audits apply, not this brief.
- Primary qualification is delivery of validated native intent/decoded samples to an application, not sound from a retail transducer. No sample-clock, FIFO-size, amplifier, safe-drive, volume or acoustic fidelity assertion. Source-only records for the original Remote do not grant physical or revision-specific Plus parity; shared codec tests may cover existing supported variants but qualify each separately.

### Protocol and consumer evidence

- Dolphin source records `audio-dolphin-wii-speaker-output-handler`, `audio-dolphin-wii-speaker-registers`, `audio-dolphin-wii-emulated-speaker`, `wii-gap-dolphin-real-core-e1d35f`: [dispatch](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/e1d35f666cc35f6ead55d32ea2679df44d04da72/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/EmuSubroutines.cpp#L362-L399), [register fields](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/e1d35f666cc35f6ead55d32ea2679df44d04da72/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/Speaker.h#L25-L65), [decoder](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/e1d35f666cc35f6ead55d32ea2679df44d04da72/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteEmu/Speaker.cpp#L22-L105), [real forwarding](https://github.com/dolphin-emu/dolphin/blob/e1d35f666cc35f6ead55d32ea2679df44d04da72/Source/Core/Core/HW/WiimoteReal/WiimoteReal.cpp#L260-L332). Keep 0x14 enable and 0x19 mute (bit 2 state, bit 1 conditional ACK, bit 0 rumble), 0x20 enable status bit 0x04, 0x16 ordinary A2 register writes. The 0x18 fixed report has shared rumble bit 0, five-bit length in bits 3–7, at most 20 encoded bytes; do not decode padding. Bits 1–2 of 0x18 and rapid-send error replies are not settled by the report struct. Preserve baseline no unconditional data ACK. Dolphin's 0x40 signed 8-bit PCM expands to signed 16-bit; 0x00 Yamaha ADPCM yields two samples per byte, high nibble first, stateful predictor and step. Nominal rates are 12,000,000/divisor (PCM) and 6,000,000/divisor (ADPCM), using the LE divisor; these are model formulas only (not the mixer's output clock or measured retail timing). Zero divisor and other formats cannot produce decoded data. A2 offset 0 is the sample path, offsets 2, 3–4, 5 hold format, divisor, volume. Preserve raw configuration; 0x40 versus 0x7f ADPCM volume conflict is not a hardware validity decision.
- Dolphin real-host forwarding is gated by its speaker setting (off by default), enable and mute; otherwise it substitutes rumble-only traffic. `audio-wii-dolphin-real-speaker-gate`, `audio-wii-dolphin-real-speaker-pacing`: host deadlines and its input-report duplication workaround do not fix the speaker packet cadence.
- **libogc WPAD/wiiuse (positive sender):** `wii-gap-speaker-libogc-startup-status`, `wii-gap-speaker-libogc-frames-pacing`, `wii-gap-speaker-libogc-stream-ack-queue-risk`; [setup](https://github.com/devkitPro/libogc/blob/ef718e2d7df5e5b1750a9e1a67c17350bdd30d3e/wiiuse/speaker.c#L65-L136), [alarm/API locators](https://github.com/anderstvoss/controller-protocol-research/blob/73a6fc6724c3fbf9b6cbbf5aaa21822978c42c4f/docs/wii-speaker-consumers.md), [queue](https://github.com/devkitPro/libogc/blob/ef718e2d7df5e5b1750a9e1a67c17350bdd30d3e/wiiuse/wiiuse.c#L295-L360), [ACK/status](https://github.com/devkitPro/libogc/blob/ef718e2d7df5e5b1750a9e1a67c17350bdd30d3e/wiiuse/events.c#L79-L159). Mute before enable; writes A20009=01, A20001=08, seven-byte A20001 configuration, A20008=01, unmute and query status. ADPCM divisor 0x07d0, volume 0x40, nominal 20-byte blocks every 6.667 ms (model 3 kHz samples). Its matching 0x22 queue-completion logic may stall because the data builder does not request ACK. This is an inference, not permission for unconditional 0x18 ACK.
- **CWiid (positive fixed-beep sender):** `wii-gap-speaker-cwiid-beep-sequence`, `wii-gap-speaker-cwiid-ack-gates`; [beep](https://github.com/abstrakraft/cwiid/blob/fadf11e89b579bcc0336a0692ac15c93785f3f82/libcwiid/command.c#L285-L347), [send](https://github.com/abstrakraft/cwiid/blob/fadf11e89b579bcc0336a0692ac15c93785f3f82/libcwiid/command.c#L53-L80), [write completion](https://github.com/abstrakraft/cwiid/blob/fadf11e89b579bcc0336a0692ac15c93785f3f82/libcwiid/command.c#L207-L281). Requires Bluetooth HID control transaction handshakes and separate memory write completion; no demonstrated per-data 0x22 wait. UHID cannot establish the Bluetooth socket handshake or native CWiid connection. Synthetic scripted replay only in S1.
- `wii-gap-speaker-cross-consumer-compatibility` is inferred consumer compatibility, not independent device corroboration. `wii-gap-speaker-cemu-wiimotelib-no-speaker-origin`, `wii-gap-speaker-upstream-wiiuse-not-originating`, `audio-wii-linux-hid-driver-does-not-send-speaker`: Cemu names IDs, WiimoteLib and separately pinned upstream wiiuse do not establish senders in the inspected scope; kernel driver has no positive speaker producer. ## Ordered steps (separate implementation PRs, each to main)

**28a. Evidence adoption and baseline/refusal contract.** After blockers clear, inventory the adopted research pin and existing Wii codec/memory/descriptor and root/registry surfaces; maintain W8 control acceptance: conditional 0x22 ACK for enable/mute, enable in status, A20000–A20009 bounded writes with ACK, reads and out-of-block/straddling writes error 7 (no prefix stored), 0x18 shared rumble even while audio is discarded. Coordinate R0/R1 without duplicating their matrix/phase-label work. If necessary add a separate research PR before the pin-adoption PR (28a), e.g. for new ACK/error, reset, volume or timing rules; no fabricated constants. Record the opted-in source model, decoder policy and ACK shim decision before 28b. **Done when:** baseline golden synthetic controls/status, disabled-mode and unsupported-realization refusal tests lock the existing wire behavior, including 0x18 oversized/short invalid lengths, rumble preservation, other Wii features, no sidecar/provider opened for rejected creation. Evidence pin or test-plan changes only as needed; no speaker support promotion.

**28b. Controller-owned speaker codec and bounded configuration.** In the Wii native module (codec/memory or dedicated speaker module), stage complete memory writes and packet decode before committing; retain raw config and separate validated format/LE divisor/volume context. Preserve existing register ACK/read/refusal semantics and independent rumble, including rejected audio portions. On the opt-in source model, decode known PCM and Yamaha ADPCM; verify length before reading, max 20 encoded bytes / 20 PCM or 40 ADPCM samples, fixed-capacity owned payload, no padding. Use independently authored synthetic vectors for signed conversion, nibble order, predictor/step clamp, state across packets and reset policy. Proposed deterministic compatibility policy pending owner answers: reject zero divisor/unknown format/invalid length as audio, preserve rumble, do not invent error ACK; muted valid frames may be accepted and decoded with explicit mute context, but never promise playback; disabled frames not audio-accepted. Preserve raw volume rather than clamp, alter divisor or infer physical gain; no rate conversion or physical clock. Set decoder state only on validated transition, no partial advance on malformed frame. Do not infer final-write latching or register reads from the setup scripts. **Done when:** exact control and ACK regressions, both setup orders, boundary/empty/oversized packet cases, invalid divisor/format, short final frame, repeated frames, mixed rumble, malformed/straddling writes, independent instances and reset/enable/mute/reconfigure cases all pass; off mode remains byte-identical. Keep feature inaccessible to apps until 28c supplies the typed journal path.

**28c. Opt-in family API and typed journal integration.** At curated and ordinary root, add the family option and typed borrowed output access in the completed VG-2 shape. Illustrative semantic variants, not frozen names: speaker enable/mute and valid A2 write controls (accepted once even for same value), speaker data accepted (native encoded count/bytes, format, divisor and nominal rational rate, raw volume, enable/mute context, rumble and bounded decoded samples), separate typed rejection/lifecycle diagnostics. A valid repeat is a new accepted command; retry of the same ordinal is not. For an invalid/disabled audio portion with accepted rumble, preserve that accepted rumble operation without falsely counting audio as accepted; keep separate diagnostic positions where appropriate. Journal speaker data only at semantic commit, never at transport admission or reply retry; retained state contains latest control metadata, not PCM; optional callback/raw observation carries no bulk speaker payload. Keep existing observer-drop counters distinct. Preflight opt-in and reviewed fixed caps before opening UHID; budget full native plus decoded bytes and metadata, including largest valid record, cap read batches. Ring pressure or absent reader cannot block protocol, change ACK or force decoder rollback; account eviction/unstored positions precisely. Application may stop, resync only at a known context or continue degraded; no automatic routing or silence insertion. **Done when:** root/curated compile, journal replay, two readers, identical repeat, retry/required ACK priority, cap saturation/exact gap, largest packet accounting, unobserved tail, stop/start continuity, reset/new epoch, close/drain and post-close refusal, no-op retained revision, independence of inputs/topology/EEPROM, and creation rollback pass. No second speaker queue, persistence, speculative USB IPC or compatibility aliases. Update affected snapshots and migration in this PR, including all compiling callers.

**28d. Use synthetic stimuli (not GPL arrays or captured traffic); virtual timing is sender scheduling and local receipt only. **Done when:** deterministic producer transcripts/queue oracles, exact conditional ACK and status, both setup orders, packet counts/byte/sample correspondence and gap reporting pass; live Dolphin run on eligible host demonstrates source-predicted forwarding and accepted data, or reports precise blocker.
